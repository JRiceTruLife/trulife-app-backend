Delete your account and app data

You can delete your account yourself in the app or in a web browser. Deletion is permanent and removes your access to saved data and purchased guides.

  1. Open Tru Life Properties and sign in with your account email and password.
  2. Open Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Enter your current password, type DELETE, then select Permanently Delete My Account and confirm.

Open account settings

What is removed

Your app login, name, email, phone, saved deals, comps history, saved project, investment preferences, reset codes and guide access are removed from the active application database immediately. A separate affiliate login using the same email is disabled and anonymized; its click history is removed.

What is retained

Transaction identifiers, purchase amounts, commission and payout records may be retained for tax, accounting, dispute and legal obligations, typically at least seven years as described in our privacy policy. These records no longer provide an active app login. Backups and records held by payment or email providers may follow separate retention schedules; contact us for a request covering those records.

Deletion does not automatically refund purchases or cancel subscriptions you manage directly with another provider. Contact the applicable provider about those separately.

If you cannot sign in

Use Forgot password on the sign-in form. You can also request deletion by emailing info@trulifeproperties.com from your account email with the subject “Tru Life account deletion request.” Do not send your password. We may verify ownership and aim to complete support-assisted requests within 30 days, subject to legally required retention.
