2. Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you give us directly, information created automatically as you use the Service, and information we receive from third parties who support the Service.

2.1 Account Information

  • Email address — required to create an account, log in, and receive transactional communications (e.g., password reset codes, purchase receipts).
  • Password — stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash (bcrypt). We never store or have access to your plaintext password.
  • Name and phone number — optional profile fields you may choose to add.

2.2 Investment & Property Analysis Data

  • Saved deals — property details, financial assumptions, and notes you enter into our deal-analysis tools, saved to your account so you can revisit them.
  • Comps Engine search history — property addresses and search parameters (bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, property type) you submit to look up sold comparables and rent estimates, along with the results returned, saved to your account history.

2.3 Purchase & Transaction Data

  • Which guides or products you have unlocked/purchased, purchase timestamps, and the amount paid.
  • Stripe checkout session and payment identifiers used to reconcile a completed payment with your account.
  • We do not collect, view, or store your full credit card number, CVV, or bank account number. All card and payment details are entered directly into Stripe's secure, PCI-compliant checkout and are processed exclusively by Stripe. See Section 6.

2.4 Affiliate Program Data (only if you join as an affiliate)

  • Name, email address, password (hashed), a public handle/slug, and your assigned commission rate(s).
  • Click-tracking data attributable to your referral links: the page visited, referring URL, and browser user-agent string — used to attribute sales and calculate commissions.
  • Sales and payout records associated with your affiliate account.
  • Banking, tax ID, and other payout details are collected and held entirely by our third-party payout processor, not by Tru Life Properties. We only see that a payout occurred and its amount.

2.5 Technical & Security Data

  • IP address — logged for rate-limiting and abuse/fraud prevention (e.g., to slow down repeated failed login or password-reset attempts).
  • Basic device/browser information (such as browser type) sent automatically as part of standard web requests.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising pixels, or cross-app/cross-site tracking technologies. See Section 7.

2.6 Information From Third Parties

When you use the Comps Engine, we query RentCast's property data API on your behalf and store the results in your account history (see Section 6). We do not receive any information about you from RentCast beyond the data responsive to your search.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create, secure, and maintain your account, and authenticate you when you log in.
  • Provide the core features of the Service: saving deals, running comps/rent-estimate searches, and unlocking purchased guides.
  • Process payments and deliver receipts and purchase confirmations.
  • Operate the affiliate program, including link attribution, commission calculation, and payout coordination.
  • Send transactional emails: password-reset codes, purchase receipts, and account/service notices. We do not send marketing email unless you separately opt in, and any such email will include an unsubscribe option.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents (e.g., rate-limiting login attempts by IP address).
  • Comply with legal obligations, enforce our Terms of Service, and resolve disputes.
  • Improve and maintain the reliability of the Service.
We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your data to serve third-party advertising.

4. Purposes & Legal Bases for Processing

Because the Service is directed to U.S. users, we rely on the following bases to process your information, consistent with U.S. privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA:

  • Performance of a contract — processing needed to create your account, deliver features you request, and fulfill purchases.
  • Legitimate business interests — securing the Service, preventing fraud, and improving reliability, balanced against your privacy interests.
  • Consent — for any optional communications you affirmatively opt into.
  • Legal compliance — where we must retain or disclose information to comply with law, regulation, or legal process.

5. How We Share Information

We disclose personal information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under contract, strictly to help operate the Service (listed in Section 6).
  • Legal & safety reasons — to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests from public authorities, or protect the rights, property, or safety of Tru Life Properties, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to standard confidentiality protections.
  • With your direction or consent — for any other purpose disclosed to you at the time of collection.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing or advertising purposes.

7. Cookies, Local Storage & Tracking

The Service does not use third-party advertising cookies, analytics pixels, or cross-site/cross-app tracking SDKs. To keep you signed in, the app stores your session token and a small list of unlocked content in your browser's or device's local storage (not a tracking cookie) — this data stays on your device and is used solely to recognize you as logged in. Clearing your browser/app storage will sign you out.

8. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained for as long as your account remains active.
  • Purchase and payout records are retained for as long as required to satisfy tax, accounting, and legal obligations (typically at least 7 years for financial records).
  • Saved deals and comps history are retained until you delete them or close your account.
  • When you request deletion of your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we are legally required or permitted to retain specific records (e.g., completed transaction records for tax purposes, or information needed to resolve disputes or enforce our agreements).

9. Data Security

We apply technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information, including:

  • Password hashing with bcrypt — plaintext passwords are never stored.
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between your device and our servers.
  • Signed, time-limited session tokens (JWT) rather than persistent shared secrets.
  • Rate-limiting on authentication endpoints to reduce brute-force and account-takeover risk.
  • Restricted administrative access, gated by owner/admin-only credentials.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately using the details in Section 17.
