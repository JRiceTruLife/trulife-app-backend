Design Templates

Pre-built design packages from our own TruLife projects — every paint color, light, tile, vanity, faucet, and finish, with vendor links and price so you can click, order, and execute.

Every finish decision on a renovation has the potential to derail your budget if you don’t have a plan before you start. These templates exist so you never find yourself purchasing off the hip — scrolling the internet at midnight trying to match a tile to a cabinet you already ordered. A complete system in place before demo starts is what keeps your project on track and your costs predictable.

Use these guides as your baseline. We encourage you to find inspiration and make it your own — that’s exactly how great finished products happen. What we’re giving you is the foundation so every decision you make from there is intentional, not reactive.

Each guide includes finished photos from the actual completed project so you can see exactly how every element looks in a real space — not a showroom. And if you’re looking for something more custom, our design specialist will schedule a call, walk through your vision, and build a package specific to your property and your buyer.
